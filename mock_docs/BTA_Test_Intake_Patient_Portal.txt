Project Name: Patient Self-Service Portal Modernization

Project Type: Digital Transformation

Project Status: New Request

Primary BTA: Jane Architecture


1. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
---------------------
Problem Statement: Patients currently rely on phone calls and in-person visits to
book appointments, request prescription refills, and pay outstanding balances. Call
center wait times average 11 minutes and 22% of calls are abandoned. The existing
portal is a legacy vendor product that is no longer supported and cannot integrate
with our new EHR.

Business Objective: Deliver a modern, mobile-friendly patient portal that lets
patients self-schedule, complete intake forms online, view lab results, message
their care team, and pay bills without staff involvement.

Business Value / ROI: Reduce inbound scheduling call volume by 35%, cut patient
no-show rates from 14% to 9%, and accelerate patient payments by an estimated
$1.4M annually through online bill pay.


2. SCOPE & REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------
Strategic Alignment: Supports the 2026 "Digital Front Door" strategic pillar and
the enterprise goal of improving patient-reported experience scores.

In Scope: Online appointment self-scheduling, digital check-in and intake forms,
secure two-way messaging, lab/result viewing, online bill pay, and SSO with the
existing patient identity provider.

Out of Scope: Telehealth video visits, provider-side scheduling template redesign,
and any changes to the billing clearinghouse.


3. TECHNICAL LANDSCAPE
----------------------

Systems Impacted: Epic EHR, Experian identity verification, Elastic payment gateway,
