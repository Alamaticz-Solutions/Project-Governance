PROJECT INVESTMENT COMMITTEE (PIC) - INVESTMENT CASE
===================================================
Project: Patient Self-Service Portal Modernization

1. CORE PROJECT DEFINITION
--------------------------
Problem or Opportunity Statement: The legacy patient portal is unsupported, cannot
integrate with the new EHR, and drives 22% of scheduling calls to abandonment.
Modernizing it unlocks patient self-service and faster collections.

Scope of Project (High Level): Enterprise-wide rollout of a cloud patient portal
covering self-scheduling, digital check-in, secure messaging, results viewing, and
online bill pay, delivered over six months with SSO to the existing identity
provider.

2. VENDOR RECOMMENDATION
-----------------------
Recommended Vendor: Bridgewell Health Digital
Vendor Justification: Lowest 3-year total cost of ownership of the three vendors
evaluated, native FHIR integration with our EHR, HITRUST certified, and an existing
enterprise MSA that shortens contracting by 8 weeks.
Additional Vendor Benefits: Includes premium 24x7 support and a dedicated
implementation architect at no additional cost for the first year.

3. PROJECT EVALUATION & BENEFIT
------------------------------
Benefit Category: Cost Reduction
Annual Benefit Year 1: 900000
Annual Benefit Year 2: 1400000
Benefit Methodology: Year 1 benefit reflects a partial-year ramp; based on a 35%
reduction in scheduling call volume (=$420K staffing), a no-show rate drop from 14%
to 9% (=$180K recovered revenue), and accelerated collections from online bill pay.
Year 2 reflects full run-rate.

4. COST PLAN & ROI
------------------
Capital Expenditure (CapEx): 480000
Net Present Value (NPV): 2100000
Internal Rate of Return (IRR): 84
Payback Period (months): 7

5. PROJECT EXECUTION & ASK
-------------------------
Key Milestones: Vendor contract signed - Oct 2026; Build & integration complete -
Jan 2027; Pilot with 2 clinics - Feb 2027; Enterprise go-live - Mar 2027.
Resource Ask: 2.5 FTE (1 PM, 1 integration engineer, 0.5 BA) plus vendor
implementation team for six months.

-------------------------
